WetPantry Scanner

A pantry that keeps track of itself. Scan a barcode to add food, photograph a recipe to see what you already have.

What it does

  • Scans barcodes and fills in the product name, brand, ingredients and allergens.
  • Tracks where food lives and when it expires, and warns you before it goes off.
  • Reads a photographed recipe and checks its ingredients against your pantry.

Your data

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your pantry and recipes are stored on your device and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Three things leave the device. A scanned barcode goes to Open Food Facts to look the product up, with nothing attached that identifies you. A photographed recipe goes to a service that reads it and returns the ingredients; that service does not keep the photo, though your own copy of it stays on your device with the recipe. And agreeing to recipe reading registers the installation with that service, so it can meter how many scans it has used. The privacy policy sets all three out in full.
