WetPantry Scanner

A pantry that keeps track of itself. Scan a barcode to add food, photograph a recipe to see what you already have.

What it does

  • Scans barcodes and fills in the product name, brand, ingredients and allergens.
  • Tracks where food lives and when it expires, and warns you before it goes off.
  • Reads a photographed recipe and checks its ingredients against your pantry.

Your data

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your pantry and recipes are stored on your device and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Two things leave the device. A scanned barcode goes to Open Food Facts to look the product up, with nothing attached that identifies you. A photographed recipe goes to a service that reads it and returns the ingredients; the photo is not kept.

Privacy policy

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Support

Questions, problems, or a barcode that comes back wrong: open an issue and I will see it. You will need a free GitHub account to post.
